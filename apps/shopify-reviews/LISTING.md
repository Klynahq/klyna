## Long description

**Turn happy customers into your best marketing — and rank for it.**

Klyna Reviews collects star and photo reviews from your storefront, gives you a
moderation workflow, and publishes structured rating data that helps search
engines understand your product ratings. It runs as a clean, fast embedded app
with a theme app block and no manual theme-code editing.

**Why merchants choose Klyna Reviews**

- ⭐ **Star + photo reviews** — a beautiful product-page widget lets customers
  rate, write, and attach photos. Real UGC is the highest-converting social proof
  you can show.
- 📩 **Launch-safe request hub** — see request status in one place. Automatic
  post-purchase email requests are not included in the launch release.
- ✅ **A moderation queue you control** — approve, reply publicly, reject, or
  flag spam. Auto-publish glowing 4–5★ verified reviews if you want zero-touch.
- 🔎 **Structured rating data (SEO/GEO)** — Klyna emits `Product` +
  `AggregateRating` JSON-LD so eligible search engines can understand your
  published ratings. Search-result display is determined by each search engine.
- 📊 **Analytics that matter** — average rating, star distribution, reviews per
  month, photo coverage, and moderation throughput.

**Built the Klyna way**

Klyna is an indie studio building open, modern SEO + GEO tools — "tools that
help your work get found." Klyna Reviews uses the same structured-data approach
that powers our wider product suite. No dark patterns and no paid API is
required for the core review workflow.

Install in two minutes, add the app block to your product page, and start
turning reviews into rankings.
