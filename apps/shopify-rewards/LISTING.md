## Long description

**Turn one-time buyers into regulars.** Klyna Rewards is a complete loyalty
program that lives inside your Shopify admin — no spreadsheets, no third-party
dashboard, no per-order surprises.

Customers earn points automatically: on every paid order, when they create an
account, when they leave a review, and when they refer a friend. They watch
their balance, tier, and referral link grow in a clean storefront widget that
matches your theme. When they're ready, their points become a real Shopify
discount code in one click.

**Why merchants choose Klyna Rewards**

- 🏅 **Points that just work** — earn on orders, signups, reviews, and
  referrals. Set your own rate (e.g. 1 point per $1) in seconds.
- 🎟️ **Redeem for real discounts** — points convert to native Shopify discount
  codes through the official API. No fake balances, no checkout hacks.
- 📈 **Tiers that drive repeat spend** — Bronze, Silver, Gold (or your own).
  Higher tiers earn faster and unlock perks, giving customers a reason to come
  back.
- 🔗 **Referrals on autopilot** — every member gets a shareable link. When a
  friend's first order lands, the advocate is rewarded automatically.
- 🛍️ **Beautiful storefront widget** — a theme app block customers actually
  use: balance, tier progress, redeemable rewards, and a copy-the-link button.
- 🔒 **Your data stays yours** — runs on your store's data and the free Shopify
  Admin API. No paid third-party services in the loop.

Set it up in minutes, then watch repeat-purchase rate climb. Built by
[Klyna](https://klyna.dev) — an indie studio making honest, modern commerce
tools.

## Permissions rationale (for the reviewer)

- `read_orders` — award points when an order is paid.
- `read_customers` / `write_customers` — enroll members and read contact
  details for the member list.
- `write_discounts` — create the discount codes customers redeem points for.
- `read_products` — display product context in future earning rules.
