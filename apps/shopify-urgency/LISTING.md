## Long description

**Klyna Urgency adds the three highest-impact conversion nudges to your store —
and keeps them honest.**

Most urgency apps fake their numbers. Klyna Urgency doesn't. Scarcity badges read
your **real Shopify inventory**. Social-proof popups are built from your **actual
recent orders**. Countdown timers do exactly what you schedule. No dark patterns,
no random "12 people are viewing this," no bloat.

### ⏱ Scheduled countdown timers

Run sale and launch countdowns on a schedule. Pick what happens when the clock
hits zero — hide the timer, hold at 00:00:00, or swap to your own message.
Evergreen mode gives each visitor a fresh per-person countdown.

### 📦 Low-stock scarcity that's actually true

"Only 4 left!" — pulled live from your inventory, per product or store-wide. Set
a threshold to start showing, and a floor to stop (so you're never stuck on
"Only 1 left"). It updates itself; you never touch a number.

### 🔔 Recently-purchased social proof

A tidy popup that rotates through real purchases: "Sarah from Austin just
bought…". Privacy-first by design — only a first name and city are ever stored or
shown. New store with no orders yet? Switch to a neutral synthetic rotation.

### 🎯 Targeting & theme blocks

Show timers by page (home, product, collection, cart) and device. Everything
installs as native **theme app blocks** — drop them in with the theme editor, no
code, no leftover snippets if you uninstall.

### 📊 Built-in analytics

See impressions, clicks, click-through rate, and attributed conversions for every
widget, rolled up by day and by type.

### Why merchants pick Klyna Urgency

- **Real data, not fake hype** — scarcity and social proof come from your store.
- **Fast & lightweight** — one tiny dependency-free script; no layout jank.
- **Privacy-safe** — first name + city only; nothing else leaves your store.
- **Free to start** — built on free infrastructure, no per-impression billing.
