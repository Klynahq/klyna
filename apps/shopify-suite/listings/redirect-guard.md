# Klyna Redirect Guard

## One-line positioning

Protect SEO traffic from deleted Shopify URLs and migration gaps.

## Merchant problem

Product deletions, handle changes, migrations, and theme launches create broken URLs. Merchants often discover the damage only after organic traffic or ad landing pages fail.

## Core features

- Samples live product, collection, and content URLs.
- Reviews Shopify URL redirect coverage.
- Flags failed sampled URLs.
- Highlights repeated redirect targets from migration cleanup.
- Scan history for agencies and store teams.

## Pricing draft

- Free: manual redirect coverage scan.
- Starter: $19/month for monitoring and scan history.
- Pro: $39/month for CSV redirect maps, agency exports, and launch QA.

## Review guardrails

Avoid ranking guarantees. Bulk redirect creation should remain explicit, previewed, and reversible.
