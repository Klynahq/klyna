# Klyna Feed Doctor

## One-line positioning

Find product data issues before Google Merchant Center rejects your catalog.

## App Store metadata

- Subtitle: Find product feed issues before channel rejection
- Search terms: Google feed, GTIN, barcode, Merchant Center, product data
- Category fit: Marketing and conversion, Store management
- Pricing: Free plan available. Pro is $9/month with a 7-day trial.

## Merchant problem

Shopping feeds fail because of missing GTIN/barcode, SKU, brand, images, variant data, or weak product metadata. Merchants need a fix queue before they replace their feed app or scale paid traffic.

## Core features

- Product and variant sample through Shopify Admin GraphQL.
- Missing GTIN/barcode, SKU, vendor/brand, image, and SEO-copy checks.
- Feed-readiness score with product and variant evidence.
- Prioritized next steps for imports, vendor uploads, and paid-shopping launches.
- Pro check history and downloadable CSV cleanup reports.
- Channel-safe language for Google Merchant Center readiness.

## Pricing

- Free: three manual checks each month, latest findings, and the feed operating guide.
- Pro: $9/month for unlimited checks, up to 50 historical scans, and CSV reports.
- Trial: seven days through Shopify Billing.

## Benefit statements

- Find missing identifiers, vendor data, images, and metadata before scaling ads.
- Turn sampled catalog issues into a focused cleanup queue.
- Export product and variant evidence for channel or agency handoff.

## Review guardrails

Do not guarantee Google approvals. Position as a readiness scanner and catalog data quality assistant.
