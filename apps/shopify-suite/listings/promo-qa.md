# Klyna Promo QA

## One-line positioning

Test Shopify discounts before campaigns go live.

## App Store metadata

- Subtitle: Find discount conflicts before campaign launch
- Search terms: discount testing, promotion QA, sale checklist, coupon conflict
- Category fit: Marketing and conversion, Store management
- Pricing: Free plan available. Pro is $9/month with a 7-day trial.

## Merchant problem

Merchants often discover discount conflicts only after customers reach checkout. Automatic discounts, codes, free shipping, markets, and expiry dates can interact in ways teams did not expect.

## Core features

- Active discount review through Shopify Admin GraphQL.
- Missing expiry-date warnings.
- Non-combinable campaign risk detection.
- Latest campaign-readiness findings and next steps.
- Fix playbook for BFCM, flash sales, and paid-traffic launches.
- Pro check history and downloadable CSV launch reports.

## Pricing

- Free: three manual checks each month, latest findings, and the promotion operating guide.
- Pro: $9/month for unlimited manual checks, up to 50 historical checks, and CSV reports.
- Trial: seven days through Shopify Billing.

## Benefit statements

- Catch missing end dates and obvious combine-rule conflicts before launch.
- Turn current Shopify discount settings into a clear preflight checklist.
- Keep evidence for campaign handoff without replacing Shopify discounts.

## Review guardrails

Position this as campaign QA, not a tool that bypasses Shopify discount rules. Do not claim unsupported discount stacking.
