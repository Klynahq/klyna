# Klyna Cleanroom

## One-line positioning

Find old app code, duplicate pixels, and theme debris before it slows your Shopify store.

## Merchant problem

Merchants uninstall Shopify apps but old scripts, snippets, app blocks, and tracking pixels often remain in the theme. They usually do not know what is safe to remove, and developer cleanup can become risky without evidence.

## Core features

- Storefront scan for common leftover app signatures.
- Duplicate Meta, Google, TikTok, Pinterest, and affiliate tracking warnings.
- External script stack count across sampled pages.
- Scan history for before/after cleanup evidence.
- Safe cleanup playbook built around backups, preview, and rollback.

## Pricing draft

- Free: one manual diagnostic scan.
- Starter: $19/month for scan history, monitoring, and cleanup exports.
- Pro: $39/month for agency-ready evidence reports and scheduled monitoring.

## Review guardrails

Do not promise guaranteed speed gains. Do not auto-edit live themes. Any future cleanup workflow must duplicate the theme, show diffs, require confirmation, and keep rollback notes.
