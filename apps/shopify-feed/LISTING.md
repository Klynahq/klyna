## Long description

**One catalog. Every channel. Always current.**

Klyna Feed turns your Shopify products into clean, channel-ready feeds and keeps
them up to date automatically. Generate a Google Shopping XML feed and Meta,
TikTok, and Pinterest CSV catalogs from the same source — with full control over
how your products are described on each.

**Why merchants pick Klyna Feed**

- **Four channels, one setup.** Google Shopping (XML) plus Meta, TikTok, and
  Pinterest (CSV), driven by the same mapping. No re-doing the work per channel.
- **Map every field your way.** Point each feed field at a product attribute, a
  metafield, or a fallback value. Set `gtin`, `brand`, `color`, `size`, condition,
  and more — exactly how the channels want them.
- **Google product taxonomy made easy.** Assign categories per collection with a
  store-wide default. No spreadsheets.
- **Per-channel include & exclude rules.** Filter by status, publish state, image
  and price presence, collections, tags, and price range. Send the right products
  to the right channel.
- **Per-product overrides via metafields.** Override any field for any product
  with a metafield — no exceptions in your mapping, no messy edge cases.
- **Always in sync.** Scheduled refresh (hourly to daily) plus instant updates the
  moment a product changes. Your feed URL never goes stale.
- **Catch problems before the channel does.** The feed health report scores your
  catalog and shows exactly which products are missing required or recommended
  fields — grouped, counted, and one click from the fix.
- **Fast, stable feed URLs.** Each feed gets a private token URL you paste straight
  into Google Merchant Center, Meta Commerce Manager, TikTok, or Pinterest.
- **Show it on your storefront.** An optional theme block adds an "Also available
  on" channel strip and consistent Product structured data.

No per-product fees. Your catalog never leaves your own app host.

Built by **klyna.dev** — tools that help your work get found.

## Support / privacy notes

- **Data handling:** Klyna Feed reads your products, inventory, and publication
  status via the Shopify Admin API to build feeds. Generated feeds are stored on
  your app host and served from private token URLs. No catalog data is sold or
  shared with third parties.
- **Scopes requested:** `read_products`, `read_inventory`, `read_publications`.
- **Support:** support@klyna.dev · docs at klyna.dev
