## Long description

**Turn browsers into subscribers — without renting another email tool.**

Klyna Capture is a popup and list-growth app that writes every opt-in straight
into your Shopify customers with email and SMS marketing consent. No syncing to
a third-party ESP, no per-impression billing, no data leaving your store.

**Build the popup that fits the moment**

- Email capture, SMS capture, or both in one form
- Spin-to-win prize wheel with weighted, fair odds per segment
- Headline, body, button, success message, and accent color — with a live
  preview as you edit
- Reveal a discount code on signup (or per wheel prize)

**Show it to the right visitor at the right time**

- Triggers: time delay, scroll depth, or exit intent (desktop and mobile)
- Targeting: by page (home, product, collection, cart), device, and new vs
  returning visitors
- Per-visitor re-show cooldown so you never nag the same shopper

**Grow a list you actually own**

- Opt-ins are created as Shopify customers with `emailMarketingConsent` and
  `smsMarketingConsent` set — they're in your marketing audience instantly
- Built-in sync status and one-click retry for any contact that didn't land
- Works with Shopify Email, Shopify Marketing automations, and any flow that
  reads customer marketing consent

**See what's actually working**

- Conversion analytics per campaign: impressions, conversions, dismissals, and
  conversion rate
- Store-wide KPIs on the dashboard

**Honest by design**

Built by an indie studio. Dismissal is always one click, cooldowns are
respected, and consent is stated plainly. No dark patterns.

## Permissions justification (for review)

- **write_customers / read_customers** — opt-ins are created as Shopify
  customers with email/SMS marketing consent; this is the core value of the
  app. Read is used to de-duplicate and check existing customers.
- **read_products** — the popup builder mirrors store branding and supports
  product-page targeting context.
- **read_themes** — required so the theme app extension (storefront popup) can
  be installed and rendered.
