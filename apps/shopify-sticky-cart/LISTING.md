## Long description

**Keep the buy button one tap away — everywhere on the product page.**

Klyna Sticky Cart adds a persistent add-to-cart bar that follows shoppers as
they scroll, so they never have to hunt for the buy button. Choose a variant,
set a quantity, and add to cart — or use one-tap quick-buy to go straight to
checkout — all without scrolling back up.

A built-in **free-shipping progress bar** shows shoppers exactly how far they
are from free delivery and updates live as the cart total changes, gently
encouraging larger orders.

Everything installs through a single theme app embed — **no theme code to
edit**, no liquid to copy-paste. Turn it on, style it to match your brand, and
you're live.

**What you get**

- 🛒 Persistent sticky add-to-cart bar on every product page
- 🎚️ Variant + quantity selectors built into the bar
- ⚡ One-tap quick-buy straight to checkout
- 🚚 Free-shipping progress bar with your own threshold + messaging
- 📱 Fully mobile-optimized (full-width on phones, compact on desktop)
- 📊 Click analytics — impressions, add-to-cart rate, quick-buy rate, top products
- 🎨 Brand controls — button label, colors, position, what the bar shows
- 🔌 One-click theme app embed — no code, works with any Online Store 2.0 theme

**Why merchants like it**

- No theme edits — enable the app embed and you're done.
- Fast and light — vanilla JS widget, no jQuery, no render-blocking scripts.
- Resilient — if anything hiccups, your native add-to-cart still works.
- Honest analytics — real interaction counts, not vanity metrics.
