Klyna is an indie studio building open, modern SEO and GEO tools. The same
TypeScript engine powers every surface: the marketing site, the browser
extension, the WordPress plugin, and the Shopify app.

## What you get

- A free **browser extension** that audits any page in one click.
- A free **WordPress plugin** that runs the same audit and auto-fixes most
  findings on your own site.
- A free **Shopify app** that brings the engine to product and collection
  pages.
- Optional **AI assistance** through a key you bring yourself, supported across
  five providers.

## What stays the same across surfaces

The audit rubric, the schema generators, the internal-linking model, and the
GEO snippet detector are all one library (`@klyna/core`). A fix to the engine
lifts every product on the same release.

## What never happens

Klyna does not proxy AI prompts through our servers, does not log your content,
and does not sell data. The free tier stays free because the core engine is
free to run.
