The Klyna AI assistant is opt-in and BYOK — bring your own key. The key lives
in your own WordPress or Shopify database, never on klyna.dev.

## Supported providers

All five are free at the tier Klyna uses by default. Pick whichever you already
have an account with.

| Provider | Free model used by default | Where to get a key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenRouter | `meta-llama/llama-3.1-8b-instruct:free` | openrouter.ai/keys |
| Groq | `llama-3.1-8b-instant` | console.groq.com/keys |
| Google Gemini | `gemini-1.5-flash` | aistudio.google.com/apikey |
| Cloudflare Workers AI | `@cf/meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct` | dash.cloudflare.com (Workers AI) |
| Ollama (local) | `llama3.1:8b` | install [ollama.com](https://ollama.com) |

## Where to paste it

- **WordPress:** Klyna → Settings → AI assistant.
- **Shopify:** Apps → Klyna → Settings → AI.
- **Extension:** Options → AI key.

## Daily cap

By default Klyna stops at 100 AI calls per day so a runaway suggestion loop
cannot drain your quota. Raise or lower it in the same settings panel
(`klyna_settings.ai_daily_cap`).

## What never gets sent

Page content goes to the provider you chose, scoped to the audit prompt only.
Klyna never sees your key, your prompt, or the response. If you delete the key
the assistant disables itself on the next page load.
