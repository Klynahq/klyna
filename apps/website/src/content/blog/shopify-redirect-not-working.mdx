## Short answer

> When a Shopify redirect is not working, do not create another rule blindly. Check whether the source path is exact, the destination is live, a theme/app layer is intercepting the URL, or the redirect is chained through an old rule.

## FAQ

### Why does my Shopify redirect still show a 404?

The source path may not match, the rule may conflict with another rule, or an app/theme route may be handling the request first.

### Should I delete and recreate the redirect?

Only after checking path format and loops. Recreating without diagnosis can hide the real issue.

### Can Search Console help?

Yes. GSC page indexing and crawl reports can show whether Google still sees old broken URLs.
