Merchants searching for **shopify discount QA checklist** usually have a live store problem, not a research assignment. This guide turns that problem into a selection and QA workflow, then explains where Klyna Promo QA fits without pretending an app can guarantee the business outcome.

## Short answer

> Shopify discount QA means testing the exact promise from landing page to cart and checkout: who qualifies, which products count, what combines, when it starts and ends, and what happens in excluded scenarios. A promotion is not ready because the discount exists in admin; it is ready when the customer-facing claim matches actual checkout behavior.

## Search demand and platform context

Search intent around Shopify promotions is fragmented across discount combinations, codes not working, free shipping, automatic discounts, and campaign checks. That makes a scenario-based QA page more useful than another generic guide to creating a discount.

Shopify groups discounts into product, order, and shipping classes and lets eligible combinations be configured. Combination behavior depends on the specific classes and rules, so the QA matrix must test both the intended winning cart and carts that should not qualify.

## What to check before choosing an app

| Check | What to inspect | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Customer, product, collection, quantity, subtotal, and market | The offer copy must describe who and what actually qualifies. |
| Combination | Product, order, shipping, automatic, and code interactions | Two valid discounts do not automatically combine. |
| Timing | Timezone, start, end, and no-expiry campaigns | A stale promotion can leak margin long after creative stops. |
| Storefront | Landing page, collection, product card, cart, and checkout | Every touchpoint should state the same offer. |
| Measurement | UTM, pixel event, order discount, and post-launch report | The team needs to separate campaign traffic from discount usage. |

## Setup and QA workflow

1. Write the promotion as a testable rule: audience, products, saving, dates, and exclusions.
2. Build a matrix with an eligible cart, boundary cart, excluded cart, and competing discount.
3. Test each scenario in the target market, currency, customer state, and mobile viewport.
4. Verify landing-page copy, product badges, cart total, checkout entry, and tracking.
5. Record screenshots, order or cart references, owner, and final go or no-go decision.
6. Schedule expiry, redirect removal, creative takedown, and post-campaign margin review.

## Common mistakes

- Testing only the cart that should receive the discount.
- Saying discounts stack when the configured classes do not combine.
- Forgetting timezone, market, customer-segment, or currency differences.
- Ending ads while leaving the automatic discount active indefinitely.

## FAQ

### Can Shopify automatic discounts and discount codes combine?

They can combine only when the discounts and their classes are configured as eligible combinations. Test the exact cart rather than assuming two active discounts will stack.

### What carts belong in a promotion QA matrix?

Include an intended winner, a cart just below the threshold, an excluded product or customer, a competing discount, a maximum-quantity case, and the markets or currencies used by the campaign.

### What should happen after a promotion ends?

Expire the discount, remove or update creative and landing-page claims, review redirects, confirm tracking labels, and compare discount cost, margin, returns, and new-customer quality.
