## Compare free popup options by the live customer path

For a first campaign, one form, one page target, and one consent decision are enough. Shopify Forms supports popup and inline forms; Shopify says a popup displays only after its theme app embed is installed. Its settings also let merchants choose the device, page type, and trigger. Check the [official popup settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/popup) before adding a second overlay app.

| Check | Safe starting point | Test on a phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer | One clear reason to subscribe | The offer fits without hiding product information |
| Consent | A visible marketing choice and data-use explanation | The choice is understandable before submission |
| Trigger | A modest delay or later page view | The popup can be closed without interrupting cart or variant selection |
| Frequency | A dismissal does not cause repeated interruption | Closing it does not make it reappear immediately |
| Success | A clear submit state and subscriber destination | The expected confirmation and record are present |

Shopify's [form settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/all-forms) explain how consent, disclaimers, and form fields work.
