## Klyna workflow

1. Classify every broken URL by source: migration, discontinued product, typo, campaign, or internal link.
2. Choose the destination that gives the visitor the same or closest job outcome.
3. Use 301s for permanent replacements and avoid redirecting everything to the homepage.
4. Review top linked 404s before low-value noise.
5. Run a live check after publishing each batch.

The important rule is simple: diagnose first, change second. Klyna Redirect Guard should be positioned as the workflow that helps a merchant or agency see the issue, understand the risk, and choose the smallest safe fix.

## FAQ

### Is redirecting to the homepage safe?

Only rarely. A homepage redirect is usually weak when the user expected a product, collection, or article.

### How many redirects can Shopify handle?

Shopify can manage many redirects, but the quality of mapping matters more than raw count.
