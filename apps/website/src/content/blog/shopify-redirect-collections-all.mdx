## Short answer

> Collection redirects need more care than simple product redirects because they affect navigation, filters, product discovery, and internal links. Map collection changes to a useful collection, not just `/collections/all`, unless that is truly the best next step.

## FAQ

### Should deleted collections redirect to all products?

Only when all products is the most useful destination. Usually a parent collection is better.

### Do collection redirects need sitemap updates?

Yes. The sitemap should expose canonical live collections, not redirecting URLs.

### What is the safest first audit?

Check source URL, destination, final status, internal links, and whether the page should be indexed.
