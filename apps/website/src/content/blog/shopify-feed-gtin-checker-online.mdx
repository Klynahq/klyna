## FAQ

### Does every Shopify product need a GTIN?

No. Google says GTIN is strongly recommended or required when the product has an assigned manufacturer identifier. Do not invent one for products that do not have it.

### Where is GTIN stored in Shopify?

For most workflows, the barcode field is the starting point, but the final feed mapping still needs to be checked.

### Which official source matters?

Use Google Merchant Center's <a href="https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324461">GTIN attribute guidance</a> for the baseline requirement.
