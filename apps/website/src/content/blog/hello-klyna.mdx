Klyna is an indie studio building modern, open-source tools for SEO and organic growth. Three products ship first — a browser extension, a WordPress plugin, and a Shopify app — all built on a single shared engine. After that, the bench gets wider.

This is the short version of why we exist.

## The problem

Growth tooling is a crowded category, but the tools have not kept up with how growth actually works in 2026. Most of what you can buy is one of three things:

1. **Enterprise dashboards** at $500–$5000/month, priced for marketing teams of 20, useless for solo founders.
2. **AI content generators** that have measurably stopped working since the helpful-content updates and the rise of detection.
3. **Single-feature plugins** that do one thing decently but require you to stitch eight of them together.

What is missing: tools that respect your time, your data, and your budget, and that *act* on what they discover rather than just reporting it.

## What we are building

Three products at launch:

- **Klyna Inspector** — a free browser extension that audits any page in one click. Pure client-side. Zero tracking. Open source.
- **Klyna SEO Suite** — a WordPress plugin that automates internal linking, schema, content freshness, and FAQ generation. Runs on your server, free tier in the WordPress.org directory.
- **Klyna for Shopify** — programmatic product page optimization, schema, and internal linking for stores. Shopify App Store, freemium.

All three run on the same TypeScript engine. A fix to the core lifts every product at once.

## The principles

1. **Free where it counts.** Free tiers do real work, not demo work. Paid tiers earn their keep with automation and scale.
2. **No paid APIs in your stack.** Everything runs on your server, in your browser, or on infrastructure you control. Your data never leaves you.
3. **Open by default.** Source available where it can be. Honest changelog. Real humans reading support emails.
4. **One brand, many tools.** Sign up once, use everything. Future products plug into the same account, the same billing, the same design system.

## What comes after

Once the first three are stable, the roadmap broadens:

- A privacy-first analytics tool
- A sitemap-driven content auditor
- An open-graph image generator for blog and product pages
- A privacy-respecting heatmap and session recorder (data stays on your server)
- An indie-friendly link-in-bio
- A static-site form backend that does not lock you in

The lineup is not random — it is the toolkit an indie maker or small e-commerce team actually needs, minus the parts already done well by others.

## Who we are

A small team. Self-funded. No venture pressure, no growth-at-all-costs, no AI-generated upsells. We are building what we wished existed, and shipping it in public.
