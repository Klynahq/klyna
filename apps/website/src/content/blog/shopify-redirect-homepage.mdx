## Short answer

> A Shopify redirect to the homepage is useful only when there is no closer replacement and the homepage genuinely answers the visitor intent. For SEO, the better path is usually old product to replacement product, old collection to closest collection, or old guide to updated guide.

## Klyna workflow

1. List the old URLs that currently point to the homepage.
2. Separate navigational noise from URLs with search or backlink value.
3. Map valuable URLs to the closest live category or product.
4. Keep homepage redirects for expired campaign URLs only when no better path exists.
5. Recheck with a redirect monitor after deployment.

## FAQ

### Is homepage redirection bad for SEO?

It can be bad when it replaces a specific page with a generic page. Use specific replacements whenever possible.

### What should I do with permanently discontinued products?

Redirect to the closest collection or an alternative product when that helps the user. Otherwise use an honest unavailable path.
