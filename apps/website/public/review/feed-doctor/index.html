Klyna Feed Doctor walkthrough

This reviewer-only page demonstrates product feed readiness checks, variant evidence, saved history, the operating guide, and Shopify-managed billing. No separate Klyna account is required.

Reviewer test path

  1. Install the app on a development store with products.
  2. Open the embedded dashboard; no separate account is required.
  3. Select Check feed readiness.
  4. Review identifier, SKU, image, and metadata evidence.
  5. Open Scan history and the operating guide.
  6. Open Plan to verify Free and Pro billing boundaries.

Core features demonstrated

  • Product and variant sampling through Shopify Admin GraphQL
  • Missing GTIN, SKU, image, vendor, and metadata checks
  • Prioritized feed-readiness score with product evidence
  • Catalog cleanup guidance and saved scan history
  • Free checks plus Pro history and CSV reports
  • Read-only diagnostics with Shopify-managed billing
