## Long description

**Klyna Wishlist turns "I'll come back to it" into a sale.**

Most shoppers leave without buying — and without an account, you have no way to
reach them again. Klyna Wishlist gives every visitor a one-tap way to save the
products they love, then quietly hands you the data to bring them back.

**Save anywhere, no login wall.**
A clean heart button sits on your product and collection pages. Guests save
instantly — no account, no friction — and their list is waiting when they
return. When a shopper does log in, their saves follow them across every device.

**Shareable wishlists that market for you.**
Shoppers can share a wishlist with a single link — perfect for gift hints,
"help me pick," and social. Every shared list points new visitors straight at
products someone already loves.

**See demand before it becomes orders.**
The Most Wishlisted report ranks the products your shoppers save most, with live
save → cart conversion. Stock smarter, merchandise the winners, and spot trends
weeks early.

**One-click install, no theme code.**
Klyna Wishlist ships as a Theme App Extension. Add the heart button and the
floating wishlist launcher from your theme editor — no Liquid edits, no
developer needed. It inherits your theme's look and your brand accent.

**Built honest.**
No paid APIs, no data resold, no dark patterns. The wishlist page lives on your
own domain through Shopify's App Proxy, and everything runs on infrastructure
you control.
