## Long description

Klyna Upsell turns the moments that already convert — the cart and the
thank-you page — into more revenue per order, without touching your theme code
or slowing your store down.

**Show the right offer at the right moment.** Build offer rules that trigger
when a specific product is in the cart, when any product from a collection is in
the cart, or when the cart subtotal crosses a threshold. Each offer recommends a
product, with your own headline, button text, and an optional discount.

**Two high-intent placements.**
- **In-cart widget** — a clean app block you drop into your cart drawer or cart
  page from the theme editor. One tap adds the recommended product.
- **Post-purchase** — a one-click upsell on the thank-you page that charges the
  existing payment method with no re-entry of card details.

**A/B test everything.** Pit two recommendations head to head, set the traffic
split, and let Klyna pick the winner once the results are real — not on a hunch.

**Analytics that tell the truth.** Impressions, accept rate, confirmed
conversions, and attributed revenue — per offer and per variant. Accepts are
reconciled against your real orders, so the revenue number is the revenue you
actually kept.

**Fast and unobtrusive.** The widget is a tiny vanilla-JS asset that fails
silently and never blocks the cart. No customer data leaves your store beyond
what the cart already exposes.
