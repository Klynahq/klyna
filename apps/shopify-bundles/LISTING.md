## Listing description

**Turn single-item carts into bigger orders.**

Klyna Bundles helps you sell more per checkout with three proven AOV levers,
all managed from one clean dashboard and shown right on your product page:

**📦 Product bundles**
Build a fixed bundle ("buy these three together") or a mix-and-match offer
("pick any 4 and save"). Search your live catalog, set quantities, and apply a
percentage or fixed discount. A real-time preview shows the exact savings your
customer sees.

**🔢 Volume & quantity discounts**
Add "buy more, save more" tiers to any product — buy 3+ save 5%, buy 10+ save
15%. The break ladder is enforced at checkout with native Shopify automatic
discounts, so the price your storefront promises is the price they pay.

**🤝 Frequently bought together**
Klyna mines your real order history for products customers buy together and
surfaces them on the product page — no setup, no guesswork, no data leaving
your store.

**🛒 Right on the product page**
A lightweight theme app extension renders your bundle, recommendations, and
volume table on the product page, with one-click "add bundle to cart." It loads
fast, matches your theme, and never blocks the page.

**📈 Revenue you can see**
The dashboard reports bundle-attributed revenue, average order value, and a
breakdown by source — bundles, volume breaks, and frequently-bought-together —
so you know exactly what's working.

**Why merchants pick Klyna**
- Native automatic discounts — savings are enforced at checkout, not faked
- No external services, no per-page billing, no data shared
- Shared pricing engine means the admin preview always matches the storefront
- Built on Shopify's modern app stack (Remix, Polaris, App Bridge)

Klyna Bundles is part of [Klyna](https://klyna.dev) — an indie studio building
honest, modern commerce tools.
