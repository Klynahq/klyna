## Long description

**Klyna Forms gives you everything a contact form should do — without a paywall.**

Most form plugins lock storage, spam filtering, and email notifications behind a "Pro" upgrade. Klyna Forms ships them in the free, open plugin, because collecting a lead shouldn't be a premium feature.

Build a form in the visual builder, embed it with a shortcode or block, and every submission is saved, spam-checked, and emailed to you — all running on your own server, with zero external services.

### Build forms in minutes

Drag-and-drop builder with nine field types: text, email, phone, URL, number, paragraph, dropdown, radio, and checkbox. Mark fields required, add options, reorder by dragging. Then embed anywhere with `[klyna_form id="12"]` or the **Klyna Form** block.

### Never lose a submission

Every entry is stored in your database and listed in the admin with unread badges. Mark read, delete, paginate, and export any form's entries to CSV in one click.

### Stop spam without a CAPTCHA

A hidden honeypot field and a server-side time-trap quietly reject bots — no annoying puzzles for your real visitors, no third-party spam service, no data shared.

### Get notified instantly

An email lands in your inbox on every submission, with the sender's address set as Reply-To so you can respond in one tap. Set a global recipient or override it per form.

### Privacy-first by default

Nothing phones home. No API key. No account. IP logging is opt-out. Your leads stay in your WordPress database, full stop.

Built by [Klyna](https://klyna.dev), an indie studio making open SEO, GEO, and growth tools.
