## Long description

**Klyna Popups turns visitors into subscribers — without renting a dashboard.**

Most popup plugins lock your email list inside a SaaS you pay for forever. Klyna
Popups does the opposite: every popup renders from your own server, every
captured email is stored in your own database, and the only outbound request is
the webhook *you* choose to configure.

**Build popups from the editor you already know.** Each popup is a post — write
the body in the editor, then set the design, trigger, and display rules in one
clean panel.

**Trigger them however converts best:**

- ⏱ After a time delay
- 📜 At a scroll depth
- 🚪 On exit intent (mouse leaving the page)
- 🖱 On click of any button or link you choose

**Target precisely:**

- Show only on the paths you pick (with `*` wildcards) — or everywhere except a
  few.
- Desktop, mobile, or both.
- New visitors, returning visitors, or everyone.
- Cap the frequency: every view, once per session, once ever, or once every N
  days.

**Own your data:**

- Captures land in a dedicated table and export to CSV in one click.
- Optional HMAC-signed webhook forwards each capture to your CRM, ESP, or
  automation as JSON.
- Per-popup impression and conversion counters with a live conversion rate.

**Privacy-respecting by default:** visitor IPs are one-way hashed (never stored
raw), there are no third-party trackers, and the plugin can honor Do Not Track.

Part of the [Klyna](https://klyna.dev) toolkit — open tools that help your work
get found.
