## Full Description

**Turn every post, page, or product into a review-powered trust machine.**

Klyna Reviews gives your WordPress site a complete customer review system — submission form, moderation queue, star ratings display, and automatic Google rich-snippet schema — all without touching an external API or paying per review.

### Why Klyna Reviews?

Most review plugins either lean on external SaaS or bloat your WordPress install with jQuery libraries. Klyna Reviews is self-hosted, schema-first, and built clean.

### Features

- **Shortcode + Block**: Drop `[klyna_reviews]` anywhere, or use the Gutenberg block — shows the form and all approved reviews.
- **5-star rating widget**: Clean, accessible star picker with aggregate score display.
- **Moderation queue**: Every review starts as pending. Approve, reject, or edit from WP Admin.
- **AggregateRating + Review JSON-LD**: Injected automatically on pages with reviews so Google can show star ratings in search.
- **Honeypot spam guard**: Keeps bots out without CAPTCHA friction.
- **Review-request email**: Optionally email users a review-request link after configurable days.
- **Lightweight**: No jQuery, no external CDN, ~8 KB of front-end assets.
