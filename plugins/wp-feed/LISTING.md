## Long description

**Klyna Product Feed** turns your WooCommerce catalog into ready-to-submit
product feeds for **Google Merchant Center** and **Meta Commerce Manager** —
and keeps them fresh automatically.

Point it at your catalog, map the fields Google and Meta care about, and copy
two public feed URLs into your ad platforms. WP-Cron rebuilds the feeds on your
schedule; the public URLs always serve the latest copy.

**What you get**

- **Google Shopping XML** — standards-compliant RSS 2.0 with the `g:` namespace.
- **Meta product CSV** — the exact column layout Commerce Manager expects.
- **Field mapping** — gtin, brand, condition, Google product category, mpn,
  and item_group_id, mapped from product meta with sitewide fallbacks.
- **Smart filtering** — include or exclude product categories, in-stock only.
- **Auto-refresh** — hourly, twice daily, or daily via WP-Cron.
- **Feed health checks** — catch missing images, prices, GTINs, and thin
  descriptions before Google or Meta disapproves them.
- **Variations done right** — variable products export as variations, grouped
  with item_group_id.
- **Token-protected feed URLs** — not guessable, rotatable in one click.

**Built honest.** Everything runs on your own server. No third-party APIs, no
API keys, no telemetry, no paid tier hiding the basics. Open source, GPL-2.0.

**Requires WooCommerce.** If WooCommerce isn't active, the plugin stays out of
the way and tells you so — it never breaks your site.
