## Long description

**Klyna Booking** turns any WordPress site into a self-hosted booking system.
Define your services, set your weekly hours, drop a form on a page, and start
taking appointments — all from one open plugin that runs entirely on your own
server. No paid APIs, no monthly fee, no jQuery bloat.

### Take bookings in four steps

1. **Add a service** — set its duration, price, and how many bookings one slot
   can hold.
2. **Set your hours** — opening times for each day of the week, plus blackout
   dates for holidays.
3. **Drop the form** — `[klyna_booking]` shortcode or the **Klyna Booking form**
   block.
4. **Get booked** — confirmation emails go out automatically; bookings land in a
   dashboard you can confirm or cancel in one click.

### Why Klyna Booking

- **Real-time slot calculation.** Open times are computed from your hours, the
  service duration, your minimum lead time, and the bookings already taken —
  re-validated server-side so a slot can't be double-booked.
- **Time-zone & DST safe.** Bookings are stored in UTC and shown in your site's
  time zone. Daylight-saving changes don't break your schedule.
- **Runs on your server.** No external service, no API keys, no data leaving
  your site. Slot maths and emails are pure PHP.
- **Light and fast.** The booking form is dependency-free vanilla JavaScript,
  loaded only on pages that use it.
- **Optional manual approval.** Vet requests first — new bookings stay *Pending*
  until you confirm them.
- **Group sessions.** Per-slot capacity supports classes and group bookings, not
  just one-to-one appointments.

### Honest about scope

Klyna Booking v0.1 is a focused appointment scheduler. It does **not** include
payments, multi-staff calendars, or customer self-service rescheduling yet —
price is informational and a booking is a reservation. We'd rather ship a small
thing that works than a big thing full of stubs. See our roadmap on
[klyna.dev](https://klyna.dev).
